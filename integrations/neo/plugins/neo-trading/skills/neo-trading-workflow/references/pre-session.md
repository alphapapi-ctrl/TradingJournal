# Neo technical pre session report

Use this format for daily preparation and the linked daily journal entry. It follows the structure of the user's historical example dated 11 June 2026. The example contributes report organisation and contextual observations, rather than replacing the PDF trading checklist.

Bookmap is excluded at this stage by explicit user instruction. Do not include a Bookmap section, request Bookmap evidence, or mark its absence as a blocker. Assess only the tools and evidence actually available.

## Report header

Record the exact instrument/contract and chart feed, account/mode, market session/exchange date, Brisbane local date, observation timestamp with offset, price area, chart timeframes and evidence sources. Keep separate reports for different instruments; do not combine Gold readings with MNQ levels.

## Market conditions

Cover current volatility, ATR, news and optional intermarket context:

- Identify the volatility measure, units, timeframe, source and observation time. A bare value such as 21.22 does not identify the indicator or establish a current volatility regime.
- Record ATR with its period, timeframe and price/tick units, distinguishing earlier readings from the current reading. Assess whether movement is sufficient for the documented setup; do not convert the example's readings into thresholds.
- Identify relevant passed and upcoming news, event time/timezone and source. A past event does not establish that the rest of today's calendar is clear. The numerical news buffer remains undefined until the user specifies one.
- Describe DXY only when available and relevant, with the observed relationship and uncertainty. Noisy or insignificant correlation is neutral context, not an independent Neo no-trade rule. Missing DXY is not a required-evidence failure.

## Market profile

Keep monthly, weekly and daily/session observations separate. Monthly can be recorded as not actionable rather than forced into a directional view. Record levels and interpretation with their actual profile period/session definition and source.

Useful context includes weekly POC, previous-session VAL/POC/VAH, current developing POC/value, whether the open was inside/outside previous value, subsequent acceptance/rejection, and an Asian-session breach/re-entry of prior extremes. Distinguish measured levels from an interpretation such as price being controlled around POC.

A poor high/low is a possible contextual reference, not a guaranteed price magnet, target or entry. POC/VAL/VAH and acceptance/rejection can organise scenario descriptions; they are not new mandatory playbook checks. If profile evidence is unavailable, record that gap and use the PDF-valid location evidence actually available.

## Price action

Describe range quality, rotations versus initiative movement, messy structure, relevant supply/demand or range edges, and where price is relative to value. Separate observation from inference and state what would improve the structure.

Wrapping around POC with no valid location/trigger can justify waiting under the existing checklist. Do not impose a universal POC ban or require a completed range when a valid PDF continuation setup is available.

## Neo dashboard and available tools

Record dashboard condition, volatility state and directional pressure separately, with their chart timeframe and time. TRADEABLE does not override STRONG BEAR/BULL or the other checklist requirements. Record ADX strength separately from +DI/-DI directional support; keep script versus panel/dashboard parameters distinct.

Describe CVD as supportive, adverse, neutral, divergent or unavailable only as supported by the actual evidence. Do not claim order-book absorption or imbalance from an absent tool. Neutral pre-session CVD means there is no confirmation yet; reassess it when a candidate forms rather than declaring the whole session invalid.

RSI/MACD need not become session-level gates. Record relevant Neo configuration when useful; do not invent a separate RSI/MACD requirement from this example. Retain ordinary 15-minute Stochastic timing in the actual PDF entry assessment, even though it was not specified in the historical example. Use the [indicator settings](indicators.md).

## Conditional session plan

Describe a long scenario and a short scenario as conditions to watch, not orders or pre-approved trades. For each, state the relevant area, required structural development, supporting pressure/CVD/timing, invalidation/structural stop concept and possible target area with sufficient space. Unknown levels remain unknown. Once an actual setup forms, apply the complete [baseline](baseline.md), including the correct Neo trigger and Stochastic check, before assessing readiness and sizing.

Typical contextual phrasing from the example:

- Long interest could develop if demand forms and price accepts above the control area with sustained buyers, supportive pressure and clean target space. A brief spike alone does not establish acceptance or continuation.
- Short interest could develop if price rejects value/control and sellers show initiative, with supportive pressure/CVD and room toward a lower reference area.
- Wait while price repeatedly rotates around control, drifts, or lacks a valid setup. Reassess when the structure changes.

Separate **PDF no-trade/review conditions** from **contextual reasons to wait now**. The former includes dead/choppy conditions, invalid location, insufficient space, adverse strong pressure, nearby major news, forced trading and a review block. The latter can include messy POC rotations or neutral CVD while waiting for a setup. DXY irrelevance alone is not a prohibition. A new filter becomes a rule only if the user explicitly adopts it.

Finish with known gaps, the demo risk basis/default or explicit override, user-reported readiness, and the next observation that would change the plan. A pre-session scenario is not an executed trade or a candidate permission.

## Journal mapping

Use the daily entry created by save_session; do not create a trade record for the long/short scenarios.

| Daily field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| pre_analysis | Instrument/date/time/source header, market conditions, profile, price action, dashboard/tools and uncertainty |
| pre_plan | Conditional long/short scenarios, PDF no-trade/review conditions, contextual waiting reasons and what would change the assessment |
| pre_risk_notes | Current balance snapshot/regime references, demo default 1% or explicit override, news and unresolved sizing/risk inputs |
| pre_psychology / pre_body_state | User responses; not provided where unanswered |
